Paying your water bill
Pay promptly
It’s important your water bill is paid as soon as possible, as CMC has already paid the full amount to Liscombe Park on your behalf.
Who pays
Responsibility for water charges remains with the property owner. Where a property is let, payment is made directly by the owner, not by a tenant or other third party.
How to pay
By electronic bank transfer only (CMC cannot accept cheque or cash), using a reference that identifies you, e.g. “No. 1 CMC Water”.
